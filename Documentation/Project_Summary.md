# Personal Finance \& Expense Analysis

## Project Summary

| Field | Content |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Project** | Personal Finance \& Expense Analysis |
| **Period** | January–March 2026 |
| **Dataset** | 19 fictional transactions |
| **Tool** | GridCraft |
| **Analysis** | Expense categories, monthly trends, payment methods |
| **Dashboard** | Completed |
| **Primary objective** | Understand spending patterns |

## Business Questions

- How much was spent during the three-month period?
- Which expense category had the highest spending?
- How did spending change month to month?
- Which payment method was used most?
- What spending patterns could be useful for financial planning?

## Key Findings

1. **Total spending** Total expenditure during the period was ₹10,626 across 19 transactions.
2. **Highest spending category** Food was the largest expense category at ₹4,460, representing approximately 42% of total spending.
3. **Monthly trend** Monthly spending increased from:
    - January → ₹3,349
    - February → ₹3,599
    - March → ₹2,428
4. *So March had the highest spending.*
5. **Payment method** UPI accounted for approximately 93.4% of total expenditure, while cash accounted for approximately 6.6%.
6. **Potential area for attention** Food represented the largest spending category, so tracking food-related purchases more closely could help identify opportunities for controlling expenditure.

## Limitations

This analysis uses a small fictional dataset created for portfolio and learning purposes. It represents only three months of transactions and therefore should not be used to draw conclusions about long-term spending behaviour. The analysis also does not include income, savings, debt, or household-level financial information.

## Recommendations

1. **Monitor food expenditure** Because food is the largest category, tracking food-related transactions regularly may help identify recurring spending patterns.
2. **Monitor monthly spending** Monthly expenses increased over the three-month period. Continuing to track monthly totals could help identify whether this is a temporary fluctuation or a persistent pattern.
3. **Maintain category-level tracking** Keeping expenses categorized makes it easier to identify where money is being spent and compare spending over time.
